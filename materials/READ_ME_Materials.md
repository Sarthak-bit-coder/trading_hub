# Materials-only package
Contains the reconstructed Part 1 course as Markdown and PDF plus five blank CSV worksheet templates. Markdown is the canonical teaching-content input; PDF duplicates it for reading. Import once, not twice.
The course includes four lessons, worked examples, assignments, quizzes, answers, checkpoints, assessment, resource library, glossary, checklist, study routine and initial strategy intake/review framework.
Later course parts are NOT included. No historical dataset, completed strategy labs, legal update, profitable backtest or complete graduation assessment is provided. CSV templates are schemas, not automated calculators.
Builder instructions and backtesting prompt are intentionally separate from this ZIP.
