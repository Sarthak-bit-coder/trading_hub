Practice

Paper trade with every cost in view.

Place long-only paper orders and see the fee, slippage, risk to your stop and reward to your target before you click.

  • Cent-accurate ledger. Cash plus positions always equals equity.
  • Stops that gap. Exits fill at the real gap price, not the stop you hoped for.
  • Journal prompts. Every fill asks what you expected and why.
Research

Backtest a rule without fooling yourself.

Load real daily data from Kraken, Alpha Vantage or CoinGecko, pick one of four published rules, and read the assumptions before the results.

  • A locked holdout. The last 20% of data stays hidden until you reveal it once.
  • Every trade explained. Each row says in plain English why it entered and exited.
  • Honest uncertainty. Monte Carlo ranges and "undefined" instead of made-up precision.
