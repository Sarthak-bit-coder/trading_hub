Liquidity, spreads, depth and market impact

The quoted spread is the gap between the best bid and the best ask. It is the cost you pay to trade right now, but it is only the first part of the cost. If your order is larger than the size shown at the best price, the rest fills at worse prices.

The effective spread

The effective spread compares your actual fill with the midpoint at the moment you sent the order. It captures what the quoted spread misses: a quote that moved, or an order that walked through several price levels.

effective spread = 2 × | fill price − midpoint |

Worked example

Best bid 100.00 and best ask 100.04, so the midpoint is 100.02. You buy 500 shares. 300 fill at 100.04 and 200 at 100.05.

Average fill
100.044
Effective spread
2 × 0.024 = 0.048
Quoted spread
0.04
Extra cost on 500 shares
$12.00 vs. the midpoint
