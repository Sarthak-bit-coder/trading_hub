Pre-trade checklist

Answer each item before a paper trade. The paper ticket shows how many you checked.

I know the product, its venue and how it settles.
My position size comes from a planned loss, not buying power.
I have a stop price and know the gap risk past it.
Fees and slippage are included in the expected result.
I wrote the reason for this trade in the journal.
This trade fits my maximum daily loss.
